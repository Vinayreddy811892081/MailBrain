Privacy Policy

MailBrain is an AI-powered email assistant that helps users summarize emails and draft replies.

What data we access

When you connect your Gmail account, MailBrain may access your email metadata and message contents needed to fetch inbox emails, generate summaries, and send replies on your behalf.

How we use data

  • Fetch inbox emails
  • Generate AI summaries and reply suggestions
  • Send replies when you explicitly choose to send them

Data storage

MailBrain does not permanently store raw email bodies unless explicitly required for app functionality. Cached AI summaries and related metadata may be stored to improve your experience.

Data sharing

MailBrain does not sell your personal data or share Gmail data with third parties except as required to operate the service.
